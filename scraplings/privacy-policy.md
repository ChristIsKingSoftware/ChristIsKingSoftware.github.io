## Summary

Scraplings doesn't collect personal information itself, and it has no account or server of its own. It shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob receives device and usage information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

## What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your device:
- banked currency;
- upgrades;
- unlocked weapons, cosmetics and checkpoints;
- whether you've seen the tutorial hints;
- when ads were last shown and which daily ad rewards you've claimed, so it can limit how often ads appear.

This file stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad between some runs, and offers optional ads you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. Ads never appear during a run.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information (model, OS version, language);
- ad interactions (views, taps).

Google's use of this data is described in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices:**
- **In the EEA, UK and Switzerland,** the game asks for your consent before personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings › Ad privacy choices**.
- **On any Android device** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

## What the game does not do

- It doesn't ask you for any permissions. (The ad SDK uses internet access and the advertising ID, which Android grants without asking.)
- It doesn't collect your name, email, contacts or precise location.
- It contains no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
- It offers no in-app purchases.

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are requested for a general audience, rated no higher than PG.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The effective date above will change. The policy will describe what is collected, why, and which third parties are involved.
